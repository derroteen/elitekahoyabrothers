How to Access the Portal

The EKB Members Portal works on any phone, tablet, or computer with internet. You do not need to download anything from the Play Store or App Store to get started.


1
Open your internet browser

On your phone, open Chrome, Safari, or any browser you normally use.

2
Type the website address

In the address bar at the top, type: elitekahoyabrothers.com and press Go or Enter.

3
The login page will appear

You will see the EKB login screen asking for your email and password.

Installing the App on Your Phone

You can install the EKB portal as an app on your phone so it opens like a normal app — no browser needed. This works on both Android and iPhone.


On Android (Chrome):
1
Open the portal in Chrome

Go to elitekahoyabrothers.com in your Chrome browser.

2
Look for the "Install App" button

At the bottom right of the screen you will see a button that says "Install App". Tap it.

3
Confirm installation

A popup will appear asking if you want to add the app. Tap "Install" or "Add".

4
Find the app on your home screen

The EKB app icon will appear on your phone's home screen. Tap it to open the portal anytime without opening the browser.

Understanding the Dashboard

After logging in, the first screen you see is your Dashboard. This is your home page and shows a summary of your account.


🏠
[Screenshot: Member Dashboard showing balances] Add screenshot here

The Dashboard shows Brought Forward Balances — these are your balances that were carried over from before the portal was launched. They are the starting point for all your calculations.


Card What it means
Savings B/F Your total savings that were brought forward from before the system started
Loan B/F The outstanding loan balance you had before the system started
Fine B/F Any fines you owed before the system started
Insurance B/F Insurance balance carried forward
Benevolent B/F Your benevolent fund balance brought forward
Note: The "B/F" means "Brought Forward." These are your historical balances — they do not change. Your current balances are shown in each module (e.g. My Savings, My Loans).

My Passbook

Your Passbook is the most complete record of your financial activity in EKB. It shows every transaction — savings, loan payments, benevolent contributions, fines, and insurance — in date order with a running balance.


1
Open the menu and tap "My Passbook"

Tap the ☰ icon at the top left, then tap My Passbook.

2
View your ledger

Your complete transaction history will load, starting from your Brought Forward Balance at the top.

3
Scroll to see all entries

Swipe left or right on the table to see all columns including Date, Description, Credit, Debit, Balance, and Loan Balance.

4
Download as PDF or Excel

Tap the PDF or Excel button at the top right to download your passbook.

Settings — Changing Your Password

For security, we strongly recommend changing your password from your phone number to something more personal and private as soon as possible.


1
Open the menu and tap "Settings"
2
Find the "Change Password" option

You will see fields for your current password and your new password.

3
Enter your current password

Type your current password (your phone number, e.g. 0721359058).

4
Enter your new password

Choose a new password that is easy for you to remember but hard for others to guess. Use at least 8 characters.

5
Confirm and save

Enter your new password again to confirm it, then tap Save or Update Password.

⚠ Remember: Write down your new password somewhere safe. If you forget it, you will need to contact the administrator to reset it for you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: I forgot my password. What do I do?

Contact the EKB administrator. They will reset your password back to your phone number and you can log in again.

Q: My balance looks wrong. What should I do?

First check your Passbook to see the full history of transactions. If you believe there is an error, contact the administrator with the specific date and amount in question.

Q: Can I make payments through the app?

No — the portal is for viewing your records only. Payments are made at the weekly meetings and recorded by the administrator.

Q: Why does my loan balance show KES 0.00 on the dashboard?

The dashboard shows your Brought Forward loan balance from before the system started. If that was zero, the card shows 0.00. Your current loan balance is shown under My Loans.

Q: The app is not loading. What do I do?

Check your internet connection. If you have internet but the app still won't load, try closing and reopening your browser or the app. If the problem continues, contact the administrator.

Q: Can I see other members' information?

No — each member can only see their own personal information. Your data is private and secure.

Q: How do I apply for a loan?

Loan applications are handled at the weekly meetings, not through the portal. Speak to the chairperson or secretary at the next meeting.

Q: Who do I contact for help?

Contact your EKB administrator directly. For technical issues with the portal, the system is supported by Dteq Solutions at infodteqsolutions@gmail.com.
